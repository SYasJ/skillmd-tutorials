# Launch page

Make ONE file, `index.html`, with all CSS inside. No frameworks, no images.

## Steps
1. Read `references/brand.md` for the exact colours and fonts.
2. Layout, top to bottom:
   - a small brand line (the product name, uppercase, letter-spaced)
   - the headline: 3 to 6 words, uppercase, very large
   - a thick green underline under the headline
   - one sentence that says what the product does
   - one yellow badge with a single number (for example "3 SPOTS FREE")
   - one button: "Get early access"
3. Centre everything. It must look good on a phone.
4. Last line of the page, small and grey: "made with a SKILL.md"

## Don't
- No white or light backgrounds.
- No purple gradients, no stock photos, no emoji.
